Test-time update auditing
Oral Presentation 32nd Chinese Medical Association Conference on Medical Informatics · 2026

A Benefit–Risk Audit of Test-Time Update Budgets for Longitudinal Medical Image Registration: A GrowthMamba Case Study

纵向医学影像配准中测试时更新预算的收益-风险审计：以 GrowthMamba 为例

We investigate whether test-time model updates are always beneficial for longitudinal medical image registration. The study jointly audits similarity gains and topology risks under distribution shift, comparing frozen inference with different test-time update budgets.

Key idea. Test-time adaptation should be treated as an auditable candidate action rather than a default deployment behavior.

My role: designed the benefit–risk audit and analyzed similarity and Jacobian-folding risk across the frozen and test-time update configurations.

  • 3D Registration
  • Test-Time Adaptation
  • Distribution Shift
  • Reliability Auditing

Shared & model-specific failures
Accepted Paper IEEE ICDM 2026 ARRL Workshop · 3D lung CT

Mining Multi-Model Failure Patterns for Reliability Auditing of 3D Lung Nodule Malignancy Predictors

A reliability auditing framework for identifying shared and model-specific failure patterns across 3D lung nodule malignancy predictors. The work focuses on understanding where medical imaging models fail together and how these systematic failure modes can inform safer model deployment.

My role: built the multi-model failure-mining pipeline and the selective-referral analysis.

Research

I study reliable learning systems for medical imaging, with an emphasis on behavior beyond the training distribution.

Research Question

How can medical AI systems remain reliable when the deployment environment differs from training?

  • When should a model adapt at test time?
  • How can systematic failure modes be identified before deployment?
  • How can multimodal evidence improve medical AI without introducing new failure modes?

Background

About

I am an MLIS student at the School of Health Management, China Medical University, working at the intersection of medical imaging and artificial intelligence.

My research focuses on 3D medical imaging, multimodal medical AI, and model reliability. I use external-cohort validation, case-level failure analysis, test-time update audits, and evidence-bounded reasoning to study model behavior under distribution shift.

Research conducted in the Department of Clinical Epidemiology, Shengjing Hospital of China Medical University.

Before graduate school, I studied at Jilin University.

I am currently preparing for PhD applications for 2027 and hope to continue working on medical imaging, multimodal learning, and trustworthy medical AI.
